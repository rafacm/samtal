# Principles (moved)

This page held vinga's product promises and its architecture
principles together. They are two different things, revised on
different occasions and by different authority, so they are now two
pages: [`product-promises.md`](product-promises.md) for the
commitments to the person running vinga, and
[`guidelines.md`](guidelines.md) for vinga's identity and the
revisable defaults that keep those commitments. The path survives
because dated records link it, and those records report what was true
when they were written rather than being rewritten to follow a move.

- [**product-promises.md**](product-promises.md): the stock-firmware
  compatibility floor, the first-class local deployment, and the
  database promise.
- [**guidelines.md**](guidelines.md): identity, thin device and smart
  server, the hardware-edge guideline, decision reasons, and what a
  pipeline framework may own.
